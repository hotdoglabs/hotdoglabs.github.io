Got a Sec? Support

Seconds in your Dynamic Island

The seconds tick inside the small pill next to the clock and stay right there, whichever app you open.

Questions

Why does it stop after 8 hours?

iPhone shows a Live Activity in the Dynamic Island for up to 8 hours at a time. That’s an iOS rule, not something Got a Sec? decided.

While it’s on, the app shows when it ends (“Showing until …”). Tap the ⓘ button next to it for the same explanation.

How do I get another 8 hours?

There are three ways to get a fresh 8 hours:

  • Open the app when 30 minutes or less are left, or after the 8 hours are up. With more time left, it simply keeps going.
  • Tap the Restart button on the Lock Screen or in the Dynamic Island (touch and hold the island first). It starts a fresh 8 hours without opening the app.
  • Tap the reminder, which arrives without a sound 30 minutes before it ends. It opens the app and gives you another 8 hours. It’s on by default; turn it on or off in the app under (···) > Remind Me 30 Min Before It Ends. It needs notifications for Got a Sec? to be on in Settings.

I can’t see the seconds

  • Got a Sec? is open. iOS doesn’t show the seconds in the Dynamic Island while Got a Sec? itself is on screen, so the app shows a preview instead. Go to the Home Screen or open another app.
  • It’s off. Below the preview, the app says “Showing until …” when it’s on. If it says “Off”, tap Turn On.
  • Live Activities are off. Turn on Live Activities in Settings > Got a Sec? to show the seconds. When they’re off, the app says so and shows an Open Settings button that takes you there.
  • Your iPhone doesn’t have a Dynamic Island. On these models, the seconds show on the Lock Screen only.
  • You swiped the card away. Swiping the Got a Sec? card away on the Lock Screen turns it off. Open the app and tap Turn On to start again.
  • 8 hours have passed. See the two questions above.

How do I change the font and color?

Tap the preview in the app, or tap (···) > Change Style…. Pick one of four fonts (Rounded, Default, Serif, Monospaced) and one of seven colors (White, Sky Blue, Mint, Yellow, Apricot, Pink, Lavender).

Your choice is saved right away. The Dynamic Island switches to it when you close the sheet or leave the app. On the Lock Screen, the time uses your font in the standard text color, so it stays readable on both light and dark backgrounds.
