Effective date: October 2, 2026

Got a Sec? ("the app") is an iPhone app by hotdoglabs ("we") that shows the current seconds in the Dynamic Island. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do. We are based in South Korea and follow Korea's Personal Information Protection Act ("PIPA"). Section 13 adds notes for Japan, Taiwan and California.

1. At a glance

  • No account, no login, no ads, no in-app purchases.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites and never use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • The seconds display and the 30-minute reminder run entirely on your iPhone.
  • Information leaves your iPhone only as (a) messages you choose to send us and (b) usage statistics, crash reports, an installation ID, an app integrity check and an update check that the app sends automatically. Google (Firebase, Gmail) processes this for us; Apple takes part in the integrity check through iOS.
  • You cannot turn off the automatic collection inside the app. Deleting the app stops it.

How these items connect. A Contact Us message stores your email (if given) with the FID, and the same FID appears in crash reports and update checks from the same copy of the app. Usage statistics use a separate Analytics ID and are not matched with messages, but they are tied to device IDs. For these reasons the App Store privacy label marks this data as "Linked to You".

About the update check. Remote Config returns one number: the oldest build we still support. If your build is older, the app shows a suggestion with Not Now and Update (opens the App Store). Every copy of the app receives the same number; we do not use personalization or A/B testing.

3. Information that stays on your iPhone

The app stores only on your iPhone: its settings and counters (display on/off, current Live Activity ID and start time, font and color, reminder setting, tip history, open and renewal counts, install date, review request stage, and the last update suggestion you dismissed), the values received from Remote Config, and Contact Us messages waiting to be sent while offline. The seconds display and the 30-minute reminder are drawn and scheduled by iOS on the device; the reminder is a local notification, not a push notification.

All of this is removed when you delete the app. One exception: Firebase keeps its installation record in the iOS keychain, which may remain after deletion; a reinstall creates a new installation ID.

7. What you can refuse

  • Contact Us and email are optional. Without a message we cannot answer you; without an email we cannot reply. Everything else keeps working.
  • Update suggestion: tap Not Now and it stays hidden until we raise the oldest build we support. The update check keeps running.
  • Automatic collection (usage statistics, crash reports, installation ID, integrity and update checks) cannot be turned off inside the app. The app uses no web cookies. To stop it, delete the app; you can then no longer use it. Information already sent is kept or deleted as section 5 describes.

8. Ads and tracking

The app shows no ads, does not use the advertising identifier, and never asks for tracking permission. We do not link your information with other companies' data for advertising or share it with data brokers. In Google Analytics, ads personalization, Google signals and data sharing are turned off. Because the app does not collect your activity across other websites or services, it does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals. Google, our service provider, collects records of your use of this app over time with device IDs (sections 2 and 6); we do not allow it to combine them with your activity on other services for ads.

9. Security

  • All connections from the app are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Message storage accepts only new messages from the genuine app (verified with App Attest through App Check), with field and length limits. No one can read, change or delete messages through the app. We view them only in the Firebase console.
  • Access to personal information is limited to the minimum number of people.
  • We review Google's terms and published security certifications to confirm that protection is at least equal to this policy.

10. Your rights

You can ask us, free of charge, to see, correct, delete or stop processing your information. A parent, guardian or authorized representative may ask for you; we may request proof of authority.

How to ask: in the app via (···) → Contact Us… (enter your email so we can reply), or by email to imhotdogboy@gmail.com. If your message included an email, write from that address. If not, tell us roughly when you sent it, your iPhone model and the text of the message.

Timing: access requests within 10 days; correction, deletion and suspension without delay. If we cannot do all of what you ask, we will tell you why. You may object to our answer by replying, or contact the agencies in section 12.

Limits: we can give you, correct or delete your messages and emails. Usage statistics are not linked to your name or email and cannot be found for one person; Google deletes them at the end of their retention period. Crash reports carry the same FID as your message, but we cannot look them up by it; Google starts deleting them after 90 days. The installation record Firebase keeps is not deleted on request. Information on your iPhone is removed when you delete the app.

We do not rely on consent for any processing, so there is no consent to withdraw. Please do not include other people's personal information in your messages.

11. Children

The app is not made for children under 14 (under 13 in the United States), and we do not knowingly collect their information. The 4+ age rating describes content only. If we learn that a child sent us a message, we delete it without delay; a parent or guardian may also ask us to (section 10).
