Effective date: October 2, 2026

  • Law: We are based in South Korea. We follow Korea's Personal Information Protection Act ("PIPA") and other laws that apply to us. Section 15 adds notes for Japan, Taiwan and California.

You can open this policy any time in the app: tap (···), then Privacy Policy.

1. At a glance

  • There are no accounts and no login.
  • The app shows no ads and does not track you. It does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), and it never asks for permission to track you.
  • The seconds display (a Live Activity) and the 30-minute reminder run on your iPhone. But when you turn the display on, renew it, or turn it off, and when you change its style, the app sends a record of that as usage statistics.
  • Information leaves your iPhone in only these ways:
    • Messages you choose to send us, through Contact Us in the app or by email.
    • What the app sends on its own: usage statistics, crash and error reports, an installation ID, and app integrity checks. Apple's servers also take part in these checks.
  • Google (Firebase and Gmail) handles this information for us, and Apple takes part in the app integrity check through iOS (section 8). We do not sell your information, and we do not give it to anyone else.
  • In this version, you cannot turn off usage statistics or crash reports inside the app. Deleting the app stops them.
  • The app has no in-app purchases.

3. Information that stays on your iPhone

The app keeps the following only on your iPhone:

  • Its settings and counters: whether you want the display on, the current Live Activity's ID and start time, your font and color, the reminder setting, whether you have turned the display on before and whether you have opened the style sheet (used to choose which tip to show), how many times you opened the app and renewed the display, the install date, and the stage of the review request.
  • Which tips you closed. Apple's TipKit stores this.
  • Contact Us messages that wait to be sent while you are offline. The Firestore software may also keep a copy of sent messages in the app's local storage.
  • The seconds display and the 30-minute reminder themselves. iOS draws the display and shows the reminder on your iPhone. The reminder is a local notification, not a push notification. No server sends data to the display, and the display itself has no internet connection. When you tap its Restart button, the app records the renewal as usage statistics (section 2).

Some of these values are also sent as usage statistics (section 2): your font and color (they are also sent with Contact Us messages), whether you want the display on, the stage of the review request, and the hours since the previous start when you renew. The Live Activity's ID and start time, the reminder setting, the tip records, the counters, and the install date are not sent.

All of this is removed when you delete the app, with one possible exception. Firebase keeps its installation record in the iOS keychain, and that record may remain after you delete the app. Based on how the Firebase software works, it should not be used again if you reinstall the app. A new installation ID is made instead.

6. How we delete it

  • When — We delete information without delay when its retention period ends, or when we handle your request to delete it.
  • Contact Us messages — On the 1st of every month, we permanently delete, in the Firebase console, every message received more than 58 days earlier. That way every message is gone within 90 days. We do not turn on backups for these messages. After we delete a message, Google erases any remaining copies from its systems within 180 days. We cannot restore a deleted message.
  • Emails — On the same day, we delete Got a Sec? emails (yours and our replies) whose handling ended more than 58 days earlier, and we empty the trash. Google may need some extra time to remove copies from its backup systems.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports — Google deletes them automatically when their retention period ends.
  • Information on your iPhone — It is removed when you delete the app. See section 3 for the one possible exception.
  • Method — We delete electronic files permanently, in a way that cannot be recovered.

10. Ads and tracking

  • The app shows no ads.
  • It does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), and it never asks for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency).
  • We do not link information from this app with information from other companies' apps or websites for advertising. We do not share it with data brokers.
  • We do not use your information for personalized ads based on your behavior.
  • In Google Analytics, we have turned off ads personalization, Google signals, and Analytics data sharing.

Do Not Track — The app does not collect information about your activity across other companies' websites or online services, so it does not respond to "Do Not Track" signals. Google, as our service provider, collects records of how you use this app and of its errors, together with device IDs, over time (sections 2 and 8). Because ads personalization, Google signals and Analytics data sharing are turned off, we do not allow Google to combine this information with your activity on other services and use it for ads.

11. How we protect your information

  • The app sends information only over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Our server rules for Contact Us messages (Firestore security rules) allow only creating new messages. They accept only the allowed fields, and they limit the message to 2,000 characters and the email to 254 characters. No one can read, change or delete messages through the app, not even the sender. We view them only in the Firebase console.
  • The message storage (Cloud Firestore) accepts requests only from the genuine app, checked with Apple's App Attest through Firebase App Check. This check applies to Firestore only.
  • We keep the number of people who can access personal information to a minimum.
  • We check that Google's terms for Cloud Firestore, Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase App Check do not let Google use the information beyond the tasks we entrust to it (section 8).

12. Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us at any time to:

  • see the information we have about you, and get a copy,
  • correct it,
  • delete it, or
  • stop processing it.

How to ask — There is no account, so there is no screen in the app to view your data. Instead, ask us in one of these ways. It is free.

  • In the app: tap (···) → Contact Us… and write your request. Please enter your email so we can reply.
  • By email: imhotdogboy@gmail.com

A parent, guardian or someone you authorize can also ask for you. We may ask them for proof that they can act for you.

How we find your message and check it is you — If you gave an email in your Contact Us message, write to us from that address; we send the results only to that address. If your message had no email, email us with roughly when you sent it, your iPhone model, and the text of the message. Once the text matches the stored message, we reply to the address you wrote from. If we cannot find it, we will tell you.

How fast — We answer requests to see your information within 10 days. We handle requests to correct, delete or stop processing without delay, and we tell you the result.

What we can and cannot delete

  • Contact Us messages and emails — We can give you a copy, correct them, or delete them.
  • The installation ID (FID) — The copy stored in your message is deleted with the message. We do not delete the installation record that Firebase keeps for this copy of the app. It is a random ID and contains no name or email. Crash and error reports linked to it are deleted after 90 days.
  • Usage statistics — They are not linked to your name or email. The app uses no user ID, and the Analytics app instance ID is not in your message. So we cannot find or delete one person's usage statistics. Google deletes them 14 months after the app was last used. To stop new collection, delete the app.
  • Information on your iPhone — Delete the app to remove it (see section 3).

If we cannot do all of what you ask, for example stopping analytics for one person, we will tell you why.

If you disagree — If you disagree with how we handled your request, reply to our answer or email us to object. We will review your objection and tell you the result without delay. You can also contact the agencies in section 14.

Consent — We do not rely on your consent for any processing, so there is no consent to withdraw. To stop all collection, delete the app.

Please do not put other people's personal information in your messages.

13. Children

The app is not made for children under 14 (under 13 in the United States). We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The App Store age rating of 4+ describes the app's content. It does not mean the app is made for young children.

If we learn that a child under 14 (under 13 in the United States) sent us a Contact Us message or an email, we delete it without delay. A parent or guardian can ask us to delete it in the ways listed in section 12.
